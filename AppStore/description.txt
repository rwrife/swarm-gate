Swarm Gate — hold the line.

Play a portrait, one-thumb zombie-wave lane-defense game built for iPhone. Stylized creatures advance in columns; aim by dragging, fire with a tap, and use a bomb when the lane gets crowded.

Choose a quick run, enter a numeric seed for a repeatable challenge, or face endless waves. Watch your health, score, wave, and firepower as the horde advances. Boss encounters and pickup decisions change each run.

Nothing is forced on you: accept or ignore each pickup. Even a power-down can affect your run only when you explicitly accept it.

After a run, see your waves, peak firepower, buffs and power-downs taken, bombs used, boss encounters, score, and personal-best comparison when a record is available. Run records stay on your device.

No accounts, cloud, ads, subscriptions, energy timers, or tracking. Swarm Gate makes no network connections. iPhone-only portrait play, accessible menus with Dynamic Type, and reduce-motion settings.
